Privacy Policy – CashCount

Effective Date: 25 October 2026

1. Introduction

CashCount is a money-counting application that helps users calculate cash totals quickly and easily.

2. Information Collection

CashCount does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer. Cash calculations are performed locally on your device.

3. Data Storage

Information entered into CashCount remains on your device, if stored. The app does not transmit this information to the developer's servers.

4. Third-Party Services

CashCount does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services, provided none are included in the app.

5. Data Security

The app is designed to perform calculations locally without sending cash-counting information to a remote server.

6. Children's Privacy

CashCount does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change.
